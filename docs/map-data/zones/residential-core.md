# The residential core (the houses): what to keep, what to remove

Same method as Villamor Air Base: the zone starts from everything the pipeline draws there now (10 Oct 2026), and this list says what goes. Numbers are for Ace to pick; nothing below is applied yet.

## What is drawn there now
- **1863 buildings**: 1795 houses (under 260 m2, under 12 m), 18 mid-rise blocks (9 to 32 m, 250 m2 or more), 0 towers (20 m or more); 1862 heights are estimates.
- **Models**: {'hangar': 32, 'mall': 1, 'hotel': 1, 'generic': 4, 'church': 1, 'school': 2}.
- **318 trees, 403 street lamps**; cars, house detail and balconies by rule wherever the buildings are.
- **Named** (5): A. Soriano Aviation (hangar, 11.4 m); Ang Dating Daan Villamor (yes, 5.8 m); Islands Transvoyager, Inc. (hangar, 10.1 m); Royal Air Hangar (hangar, 10.6 m); Topflite Airways, Inc. (hangar, 4.3 m)

## Remove (proposed)
1. **Air-conditioners and dishes on the houses**: invented and noisy at this density.
2. **Chimneys on the pitched roofs**: Philippine houses mostly have none.
3. **Water tanks and stair heads on a share of the flat roofs**: keep on one in three, not on every house.
4. **Awnings on every small flat block**: keep only where the block is tagged a shop.
5. **Cars parked on the narrow lanes** (they overlap houses at 1.5 m): keep the main streets.

## Keep
Doors and windows on the longest wall, the roof rims, the pitched roofs, the 3,000 or so house footprints.
