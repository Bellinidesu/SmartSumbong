## Remove (proposed)
1. **Cars on the airfield service roads and the car parks** apart from the terminal's kerb.
2. **Street lamps along the airfield roads** (the airfield has its own lights).
3. **Rule detail on the hangars** beyond the door and ribs (rim, tanks, dishes).
4. **Parked airliners on stands that are not at Terminal 3** (keep the jet-bridge gates only).

## Keep
Terminal 3 itself, the runway and taxiway markings, the jet bridges, the control tower and the mast.
